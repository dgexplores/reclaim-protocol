### Slide 2: Problem

- Result: $55/ton landfill cost, missed ESG mandates

- Cities: pay landfill fees

### Slide 5: How It Works (3 steps)
1. **Scan** — AI on-device (92% accuracy, 6 materials)
2. **Prove** — Image hash + IPFS CID on-chain, anti-gaming checks
3. **Earn** — Material-weighted tokens, redeemable

### Slide 6: Why Now / Why Web3
- Base L2 fees <$0.01, IPFS cheap, TFLite on phone
- EU Green Claims Directive requires verifiable proof
- DePIN narrative hot — phones as oracles
- Composability: tokens → DeFi, NFTs → reputation

### Slide 7: Unique Tech
- Proof-of-Recycling (not PoW)
- Anti-gaming: on-chain image-hash dedup, an 85% confidence floor enforced in Solidity, and staked auditors whose bonds are slashed on a wrong challenge. Audit selection is deterministic on the image hash and publicly recomputable, not a VRF
- On-device AI, privacy-preserving

- Frontend classifies live, IPFS working
- No pilot has run yet. 34 contract tests pass and the scan to mint path works end to end on testnet
